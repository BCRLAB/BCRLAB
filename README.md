## 🧬 About Us
**BioComp Research Lab** is an interdisciplinary academic research group dedicated to bridging massive biological datasets with advanced artificial intelligence and computational architectures. We focus on uncovering hidden molecular patterns, biomarker signatures, and disease mechanisms to advance precision medicine.

* **Website:** [bcrlab.ir](https://bcrlab.ir)
* **Lead Researcher:** [Dr. Siamak Salimy](https://github.com/Siamak-salimy)

---

## 🔬 Core Research Interests
* **Cancer Genomics & Multi-omics:** Integrating genomics, transcriptomics, and clinical cohorts (e.g., TCGA) to study cancer subtypes and patient outcomes.
* **Machine Learning & AI in Biology:** Applying deep learning and classical ML models to high-throughput biomedical and omics data.
* **Bioinformatics Software & Tools:** Developing robust, open-source software and tools for genomic data retrieval and computational pipelines.
* **Computational Biology:** Modeling biological systems and molecular pathways using network-based approaches.

---

## 🚀 Featured Projects & Tools
* **[HMNexus](https://github.com/Siamak-salimy/HMNexus):** A desktop application designed to streamline the retrieval and download of Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA) datasets without command-line hurdles.
